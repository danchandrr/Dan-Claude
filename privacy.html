Privacy notice

Zebra Shift · plain-language summary of what the game stores

Playing without an account

Your careers, settings and Case of the day results are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you sign in for friends and leaderboards

  • Your email address is used only to sign you in. It is held by the sign-in service and is never shown to other players.
  • Public to other players: your username, your doctor's name and portrait, your specialty, your best score, Casebook count, shifts played, emergencies handled, number of achievements and your Case of the day scores.
  • Private to you: a copy of your careers, so you can carry on from another device; your friend requests and challenges, which only you and the other player can see; and, if you turn on reminders, the address your device gives for notifications plus the hour you chose.

If you use Report a problem on a case or picture, the game sends the case name, the kind of problem, your note and, if you are signed in, your username. Only the developer can read reports.

The data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the game's database. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.

Deleting your account

Open Friends & leaderboards → Account → Delete my account. This removes your profile, scores, friends and cloud saves straight away. Saves on your own device stay until you delete them from the Load career screen.

Medical content

Zebra Shift is a game for education and entertainment. It is not clinical guidance, and nothing you enter is health information about you.
